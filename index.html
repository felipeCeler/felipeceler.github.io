I am Felipe Moura de Carvalho, a software developer and researcher skilled in Computer Graphics and Visualization. Throughout my career, I have collaborated with multidisciplinary teams across humanities, engineering, and scientific domains. Professionally, I started my career as a researcher and developer working as a Postdoctoral Associate at the University of Calgary on The Rapid Reservoir Modeling project, where I applied visualization techniques and natural user interfaces. Then I made a transition to the industry and worked as a Software Engineer at the Tecgraf Institute, focusing on optimizing 3D data exchange and maintaining a Digital Twin visualization software.

My academic background is rooted in Computer Science with an emphasis in Computer Graphics. I studied at the Federal University of Piauí for my bachelor's degree before moving on to the Federal University of Rio de Janeiro, specifically at the Institute Alberto Luiz Coimbra of Graduate and Research in Engineering (COPPE) ,where I obtained both my M.Sc. and Ph.D. in Systems Engineering and Computer Science. My core technical expertise spans illustrative visualization, GPU programming, computational geometry, mesh processing, and natural interfaces.

Looking forward, my career is driven by problem-solving and continuous learning in software development. Currently, I am expanding my expertise in Qt/QML, Vulkan and modern C++, development through dedicated side projects to continually refine my technical skillset.
