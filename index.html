I am Felipe Moura de Carvalho, a software developer and researcher skilled in Computer Graphics and 3D Visualization. Throughout my career, I have collaborated with multidisciplinary teams across humanities, engineering, and scientific domains, translating complex technical requirements into reliable software solutions. Professionally, I have contributed as a researcher and developer working as a Postdoctoral Researcher at the University of Calgary on The Rapid Reservoir Modeling (2015-2019), where I applied visualization techniques and natural user interface. I made a transition to the industry and started work as Software Engineer at the Tecgraf Institute (2020-2025), focusing on optimizing 3D data exchange and maintaining a Digital Twin visualization software.

My academic background is rooted in Computer Science with emphasis in Computer Graphics. I studied at the Federal University of Piauí for my bachelor's degree before moving on to the Federal University of Rio de Janeiro, specifically at the Institute Alberto Luiz Coimbra of Graduation and Research in Engineering (COPPE), where I obtained both my M.Sc. and Ph.D. in Systems Engineering and Computer Science. My core technical expertise spans illustrative visualization, GPU programming, computational geometry, mesh processing and natural interfaces.

Looking forward, my career is driven by problem-solving and continuous learning in software development. I continue sharpening my skill on solutions where modern C++ is required, particularly drawn to both front-end and back-end challenges, with a strong focus on data visualization and user interactions. Building on my background in multidisciplinary research, I aim to continue creating high-impact visualization systems and innovative software solutions that deliver intuitive and powerful user experiences.
